  1. Problem
    Crop disease drives major yield loss, yet accurate detection models are usually too heavy for the low-end hardware farmers actually have, and out of reach for non-English speakers.
    Approach

    Result
    98.94% validation accuracy and about 98% on the held-out test set, light enough for low-power devices.
    Stack
    PyTorch, Swin Transformer, React, Django

  2. Problem
    Which policies in an insurance book actually make money, and how badly does claim frequency erode it?
    Approach
    Generated policy and claims data, then used SQL and Pandas to compute premium revenue, claim-cost trends, loss ratios by tenure and projected liabilities under different claim-frequency assumptions.
    Result
    Three-year policies are the most profitable (lowest loss ratio, 1.31). The portfolio as a whole pays out more in claims than it earns in premium, and higher claim frequency makes it sharply worse.
    Stack
    Python, Pandas, SQLite, Seaborn
